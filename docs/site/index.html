Three steps to the ring

Your game. Your local installation.

The first launch verifies your supported disc image and prepares everything locally. Keep at least 5 GiB free, plus room for caches.

01 / UNPACK

Extract the Linux release

Download beta 0.7 from GitHub Releases and extract it into a new folder. Keep your original disc image separate.

02 / LAUNCH

Run ./launch.sh

Open a terminal in the extracted folder and start the launcher. First-run setup opens automatically.

03 / SELECT

Choose your own ISO

Select the supported USA edition when prompted. Setup verifies and extracts it, then starts the game. Existing extracted data is also supported.

Built into the game

Set up your corner.

Native OPTIONS → GRAPHICS

Resolution, fullscreen, FXAA and texture filtering. Save your settings or use SAVE AND RESTART. Original MSAA remains read-only.

Vulkan with Mailbox/VSync

Persistent shader caching and the original game clock. Higher resolutions depend on your GPU; 900p, 1080p and 1440p share the 2× render tier.

Prompts that follow your device

Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo Switch families, automatic last-device tracking, manual selection and 14 optional action bindings. The Ally's integrated controls have been physically tested; other families await full device verification.

Before the bell

A beta with clear limits.

  • Recent Linux x86-64 with glibc 2.38+ and GLIBCXX_3.4.32 or newer in libstdc++ (GCC 13.2).
  • Working Vulkan graphics drivers, X11 or Wayland, and the system libraries listed in the installation guide.
  • Python 3.12+, your supported USA disc image and at least 5 GiB of free space recommended.
  • Linux gameplay has been tested on the ROG Ally. Windows is source support pending validation.
A complete fight and prolonged audio stability are still unverified. Intermittent loading, text and audio issues remain. Experimental 60 FPS and interpolation are outside this release.
